# STUDYAI — Privacy Policy for Students, Guardians & Educational Institutions

**Last Updated:** November 2026  
**Effective Date:** November 2026  
**Applicability:** Global (COPPA, FERPA, GDPR-K, India Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023)

---

## 1. Introduction & Core Privacy Principles
STUDYAI ("we", "our", or "platform") provides an advanced multimodal educational learning ecosystem for students. We believe student educational data is strictly confidential and must never be commercialized, sold, or used for behavioral advertising.

## 2. Information We Collect
1. **Account Credentials**: Name, school email address, grade level, and school/institution affiliation.
2. **Academic & Learning Telemetry**: Topics studied, question responses, examination scores, practice streak, time spent on lessons, and gamified mastery points.
3. **Student-Authored Content**: AI study notes, flashcards created, uploaded diagrams/worksheets, and questions asked to the AI Tutor.
4. **Device Telemetry**: Device operating system version, app version, network latency, and crash telemetry to ensure uptime and platform stability.

## 3. Strict Student Confidentiality & Parent Access Boundaries
- **Parent Portal Scope**: Verified parents/guardians can inspect their linked child's attendance, examination scores, subject completion percentages, and tuition ledger.
- **Student Private Conversation Isolation**: In accordance with student autonomy and educational safety guidelines, raw exploratory conversations between a student and the AI Tutor are kept strictly confidential by default and are not broadcast to parents unless explicit safeguarding alerts are triggered.

## 4. No Third-Party Commercial Tracking & No Advertising
- **Zero Ads**: STUDYAI contains no behavioral advertisements, third-party marketing trackers, or ad-retargeting pixels.
- **Zero Data Brokers**: We do not sell, rent, or lease student information to any data broker or advertiser.

## 5. Artificial Intelligence Processing & Model Training Safeguards
- Queries submitted to the AI Tutor are processed solely to synthesize educational explanations, labeled diagrams, and 3D simulation mappings.
- Student personal identification information (PII) is scrubbed prior to any LLM inferencing.
- We do not use student-generated private notebooks or test responses to train public foundation models without explicit institutional consent.

## 6. Data Retention & Deletion Rights
Students, parents, and school administrators possess the right to:
- Export all personal study notes in Markdown, JSON, or standalone HTML format.
- Request complete deletion of their student profile and historical examination logs.
- Data retention adheres to institutional educational compliance requirements.
