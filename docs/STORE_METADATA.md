### Full Promotional Description
Transform how you learn with STUDYAI, an advanced educational ecosystem that teaches topics visually, structurally, and interactively.

STUDYAI is not a simple chatbot. It is a comprehensive AI learning architect designed to guide you through a complete mastery loop:

• **Pedagogical AI Tutor**: Breaks complex concepts into logical subtopics, learning objectives, full terminology breakdowns, and intuitive analogies.
• **Interactive 3D Simulation Lab**: Explore Three.js 3D models of the human heart, plant cells, atomic orbitals, the solar system, and DNA with 360° rotation and cross-sections.
• **High-Contrast Concept Diagrams**: Clear, labeled SVG vector diagrams that clarify complex processes without distracting clutter.
• **Intelligent AI Notes**: Convert topics, PDFs, textbook photographs, and recorded lessons into structured study notebooks with auto-generated flashcards and practice quizzes.
• **Adaptive Educational Arcades**: Reinforce subject mastery through Concept Duel, Astro Interceptor 3D, and Concept Path Runner.
• **Authoritative Examination Suite**: Timed weekly tests covering all 8 curriculum question types with automatic evaluation and personalized weak-area recommendations.
• **Privacy by Design**: Zero commercial advertising, zero data brokers, and complete student-first data isolation.
